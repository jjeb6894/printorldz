Free & Open-Source Downloads

Our policy: We only list software here that is genuinely Freeware, Open Source, or offers a real Free Tier. Every link below goes to the official vendor or project site — never a mirror, file-locker, or third-party host. We do not host installers ourselves, and we do not provide license keys, key generators, or cracks for paid software of any kind.

Software Type Notes Official Link
TurboCNC Freeware DOS-based CNC motion control software released as freeware by its author, Art Fenerty. dakeng.com/turbo.html
LinuxCNC Open Source Full CNC motion control software stack for Linux (successor to EMC2), released under the GPL. linuxcnc.org
GRBL Open Source Open-source G-code interpreter/motion control firmware for Arduino-class microcontrollers. github.com/gnea/grbl
KCam (v4 Free Edition) Freeware Kellyware historically offered a free edition of KCam alongside its paid "Pro" version. kellyware.com
Mach3 (Demo Mode) Free Tier Official demo runs indefinitely but is limited to ~500 lines of G-code — not a full license. machsupport.com/software/mach3
Fusion 360 (Personal Use) Free Tier Autodesk offers a free personal-use / hobbyist license with feature and commercial-use limits. autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/personal
FreeCAD Open Source Fully open-source parametric 3D CAD/CAM modeler with a built-in Path workbench for CNC. freecad.org
DraftSight (Historical Free Edition) Freeware (historical) Dassault Systèmes offered a free 2D CAD edition for several years before moving to a subscription-only model. Check the official site for current availability. 3ds.com/products/draftsight

A Note on Scope

We do not list downloads for software that is normally paid unless the developer/vendor has made it genuinely free (freeware), open-source, or offers an official free tier. Items marked "historical" may have changed licensing since we last checked — always verify current terms on the official site before downloading.
