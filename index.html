Welcome to Printorldz!

Printorldz is a hobbyist-run information portal dedicated to CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machines and the CAM (Computer-Aided Manufacturing) software that drives them. Whether you're running a brand-new Tormach in your garage or you dusted off an old Bridgeport with a retrofit controller, this site is your reference point for figuring out what's out there — past and present.

We started cataloging CNC machines and CAM/CNC control software because so much of the history of this hobby/industry is scattered across forums, dead links, and old newsgroup posts. Our goal: keep a simple, fast-loading, no-nonsense reference that works even on old hardware.

What's Inside

  • CNC Machines & Software Catalog — big reference table of well-known machine brands/models and CAM/CNC control software, with era and status info.
  • Downloads — legitimately free/freeware/open-source CNC software only, linked straight to the official source.
  • Store — a few first-party Printorldz guides and reference sheets we made ourselves.
  • About / Contact — who runs this thing and how to reach us.
